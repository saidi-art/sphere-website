Delete your Sphere account

  1. Open Sphere and sign in The fastest way is inside the app.
  2. Go to Settings Tap Delete account and confirm. You may be asked to sign in again to prove it is you.
  3. Done Your Sphere account is permanently deleted from the authentication service (Firebase Authentication, by Google). Google then removes the deleted account's data from its live and backup systems within 180 days.

Your captures are not affected

Recordings, screenshots and texts are files on your phone in Download/Sphere, and stay there until you delete them yourself. Deleting the app does not delete these files either.

No access to the app?

Email saidi@kubeai.info from the address your account uses with the subject "Delete my Sphere account" and we will delete it for you.
