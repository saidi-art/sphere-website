Sphere by KubeAI, a floating capture tool for Android

Capture here, switch, drop there.

A small sphere floats over your other apps. Tap it to record your screen. Drag it to the bottom bar and it auto-scrolls the page for you, reading as it goes, hands-free. Sphere turns what it captures into searchable text on your phone, saved in your own folder under Download/Sphere.

Everything is read on your phone. Captures are never uploaded; you choose whether to share anything. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

A whole thread, tap and leave.

Rescue scrolls back to the first message and captures every one after it while you get on with something else.

Sphere hands over exactly what it captured: no rewriting, no summaries. Where a line cannot be proven, it says so. Direct text, which reads the screen through Android's accessibility API instead of the pixels, is experimental and off by default.

A new colour every day

Day theme is generated, not chosen from a set. The sphere's colour comes from the day of the year: its hue is the day number times the golden angle (137.50776 degrees), drawn in OKLCH, so each of the 365 days of the year has its own colour. No two days look the same, and the same date looks the same every year.

Night desk is a fixed material set: emerald cased glass and polished brass, like a banker's lamp on a dark desk. It never changes by day. The dial above shows today's palette at the shell's real offsets: Rescue 120 degrees away, Auto-scroll 240, the record button 300, Tools the complement at 180, softened.
