Sphere by KubeAI

A floating capture tool for Android.

A small sphere floats over your other apps. Tap it to record your screen. Drag it to the bottom bar and it auto-scrolls the page for you, reading as it goes, hands-free. Sphere turns what it captures into searchable text on your phone, saved in your own folder under Download/Sphere.

Everything is read on your phone. Captures are never uploaded; you choose whether to share anything. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

A new colour every day

Day theme is generated, not chosen from a set. The sphere's colour comes from the day of the year: its hue is the day number times the golden angle (137.50776 degrees; a rounded 137.5 would repeat every 144 days), drawn in OKLCH, so each of the 365 days of the year has its own colour. No two days look the same, and the same date looks the same every year.

The rest of the shell follows that hue at fixed offsets: Rescue sits 120 degrees away, Auto-scroll 240, the record button 300, and the small tools take the complement at 180, softened.

Night desk is a fixed material set: emerald cased glass and polished brass, like a banker's lamp on a dark desk. It never changes by day. Follow the phone maps light mode to Day and dark mode to Night desk, and the re-theme applies the moment you pick.

Honest by design

Sphere hands over exactly what it captured: no rewriting, no summaries. Where a line cannot be proven, it says so. Direct text, which reads the screen through Android's accessibility API instead of the pixels, is experimental and off by default.

Your account

Sphere requires an account before you can capture. Sign-in runs on Firebase Authentication; your captures themselves stay on your phone. You can delete your account from Settings at any time.
